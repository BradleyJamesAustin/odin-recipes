also known as garlic soup!

Here is what youl need!

  • Garlic Bulbs x3

  • Water
  • salt

How to make the anti vamp juice

  1. Chop the hell out of the onions
  2. Peel and chop garlic
  3. Boil stock
  4. Mix it all together
  5. Use a blender or fork to mash it all up
  6. Drink it with a straw
  7. Live vamp free!!!
